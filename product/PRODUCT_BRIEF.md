# Product Brief — Songbird

## Purpose and origin
Songbird helps people bring songs they have carried in their hearts into the world. The emotional core includes a longtime musician with unfinished melodies, older family hymn recordings worth preserving, an adult wanting to create without studio equipment or collaborators, and children exploring music through play. Human creation comes first; AI is the producer and assistant, not a replacement for the creator.

## Product promise
**Make it yourself. Make it sound incredible. Bring it to life.**

People hum, sing, clap, beatbox, tap, speak rhythm, play an instrument, or import a recording. Every input becomes a separately editable and loopable layer. Songbird detects musical intent, can tune/clean the original or transform it into an instrument/ensemble, helps arrange and mix the composition, masters and exports it, and optionally creates a music video from prompts, photos, and performance footage. Creators save, revisit, share privately or with family, and optionally publish publicly under explicit rights controls.

## Jobs to be done
- Turn a hummed melody into a polished arrangement without knowing instruments or music theory.
- Make a beat by clapping or beatboxing and convert it to a synchronized drum kit.
- Perform live through a virtual instrument with optional real-time monitoring.
- Clean a recording with a single intelligent fix, guided by understandable explanations.
- Restore and preserve a loved one's authentic recorded voice.
- Create together across generations and revisit unfinished sessions.
- Produce a high-fidelity audio release and an optional visualizer, photo film, or AI video.

## Differentiation
1. Human-first input, not prompt-first music generation.
2. Layer-first, non-destructive creation; preserve source melody, rhythm and phrasing.
3. Intelligent producer with *Magic Log* and *Magic Fix* explaining and reversing decisions.
4. Same engine for child-friendly guided mode and experienced musician independent mode.
5. Live transformer plus higher-quality offline rendering.
6. Family Songbook, archival voice preservation, and granular share/remix permissions.

## Audience and access
Primary: families and parents, kids creating with guardian controls, seniors and hobbyist musicians with unfinished songs, gift buyers making legacy keepsakes. Secondary: amateur songwriters, church/community musicians, teachers, and creators who want portable production. Never assume a user's ability from age. Offer Guided and Independent preferences, large controls, spoken guidance, and optional advanced inspector.

## Success measures
- Time to first recorded layer; time to first transformed layer.
- % new users completing a two-layer song; % returning to saved sessions.
- % recommendations previewed, accepted, reverted; perceived improvement in blinded listening tests.
- Recording/transform failures; loop drift; median audio-to-preview latency; live round-trip latency per device.
- Completion, share, export and package conversion; cost per rendered minute; support tickets.

## Non-goals
Do not automatically invent a song and attribute it to the user; do not overwrite original recordings; do not claim all generated elements have guaranteed copyright; do not make public sharing mandatory; do not prioritize a social feed over the creation engine.
